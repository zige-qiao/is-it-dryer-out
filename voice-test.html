Audio interruption test

Start a recording and tap Mark before switching. Switch to another app, start and stop some audio, then return and tap Mark return. This mode does not stop recognition just because this page is hidden; iOS may still suspend it. The attempt is limited to 30 seconds. Logs include browser and recognition events, never your transcript.

Microphone reset test

After a recording fails to hear speech, tap Reset microphone. This briefly opens and releases a standard microphone stream. When the reset completes, tap Start and speak again. The log records microphone and recognition events, never your transcript.

Held microphone stream test

Start a recording and speak normally. Held mode opens a standard microphone stream first and releases it after recognition ends. Persistent manual-stop mode retains the live stream and waveform after Stop. Track pause mode instead immediately disables the retained audio track and pauses the waveform after Stop or Abort, then re-enables that same track for the next attempt. A disabled track is not proof that microphone hardware or capture has stopped: observe the iPhone microphone indicator and include what it does in your report. Natural completion and the Release microphone button fully release resources; a paused stream is also released after 30 seconds.
