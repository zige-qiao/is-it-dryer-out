Ventilation Timer

The Shortcut installer is not available in this development build yet. Nothing is downloading or being installed on your phone.

The developer must sign and provide the installer first. Once available, you will add one ready-made Shortcut; you will not need to build or edit its actions.

After installation, keep its name as Ventilation Timer. The app supplies a different duration each time you start a timer.

After installation

Test a 1-minute timer. Check that it appears in Clock, then test its alarm with the phone locked and offline. Check the Clock timer's sound is an audible tone, rather than Stop Playing.

The app cannot detect installation or confirm that a timer started. Check Clock after the first handoff.
