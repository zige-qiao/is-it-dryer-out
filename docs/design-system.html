Universal gesture rule · swipe without activation

Upward, downward, horizontal and diagonal swipes or drags must never also activate a tap action. This applies to page controls, switches, sheet headers/footers, links and disclosures.

Swipe or drag

Scroll or perform the intended component gesture. Movement of 8 CSS pixels or cancellation suppresses the resulting click, even after returning to the starting point.

Tap to stop scrolling

Stop movement without toggling the underlying control. A new tap after scrolling settles activates normally.

Normal activation

Settled taps, mouse clicks, keyboard and assistive activation remain available.

Intentional gestures

Preserve chart inspection, rulers, camera crops, reorder handles and sheet/pull dismissal. Never disable every control or intercept native scrolling.

Reorder feedback · press, ready, move and settle

Press: subtle handle surface; early swipes scroll. Ready: at 300ms, dark grip and soft row highlight. Move: an inert, full-opacity preview follows vertically; neighbours slide into a measured gap. Drop: save once and settle in 160ms. Cancel: return to the original slot without saving. No scale jump, thick marker or faded row. Reduced motion preserves finger tracking with immediate settling; forced colours outline the preview and gap.

Glossary
AH Absolute humidity
The amount of water vapour in the air, measured in grams per cubic metre (g/m³). Lower outdoor AH means outdoor air contains less moisture per cubic metre than indoor air.
RH Relative humidity
How close air is to saturation at its current temperature, shown as a percentage. Warming air lowers its RH without removing water vapour.
DP Dew point
The temperature at which air becomes saturated when cooled. Condensation can form on surfaces at or below this temperature.
ACH Air changes per hour
The hourly volume of incoming air divided by the room volume. For example, 2 ACH means airflow equivalent to twice the room’s volume each hour; it does not mean all the original air is replaced.
Let in
The estimated RH of outdoor air once it reaches your current indoor temperature.
m³ Cubic metres
A measure of room volume: length × width × height in metres.
How estimates work
  • Opening times are estimates. They depend on room size, how far the windows are open, and the weather.
  • Furniture, fabrics and walls can release moisture as the air dries, so humidity may fall more slowly than estimated.
  • Cooling can keep the humidity reading high even while moisture is removed. The reading may fall as the room warms again.
  • Take fresh indoor readings during ventilation. Close the windows sooner if the room gets too cold.
  • Future forecasts assume your indoor readings stay the same until that time.
  • The chart line shows outdoor moisture against your indoor reading. Bars show estimated air changes per hour (ACH), not measured airflow.
  • Higher humidity makes condensation more likely on cold windows and walls. The app checks whether room air reaches 100% humidity, but does not predict condensation on those colder surfaces.

Geometry rules

  • Fixed spacing and sizes follow a 4px grid: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 32px. Typography, fine strokes, focus rings, and fluid/data-driven geometry are independent.
  • 44px minimum action target; 48px primary action
  • 52px numeric field height; paired ruler fields and Indoor RH 100px wide; Indoor temperature 128px wide, with non-shrinking inputs and 4px extra numeral space
  • 20px outer dashboard cards; 16px inner comparison cards; 24px sheets
  • Forecast tabs: 6px inner radius inside an 8px outer track, an explicit 4px-grid exception
  • 1px ordinary border; 2px selection border; 2px outer focus ring with a 2px gap
  • No decorative shadows
  • 120ms control response; respect reduced motion

Behaviour is part of the system

Manual edits save immediately; the header tick closes. Voice changes require review and Apply. Rulers support typing, dragging and arrow keys. Sheets contain their scroll, adapt to the visible viewport, and return focus on dismissal.

Preserve the current centred 496px maximum app shell. Location, voice, custom panels, loading, errors and disabled states are specified in the full written system.
