## Manual readings

Indoor readings opens on initial launch into Overview by default; opening Why directly does not open it. Enter temperature and relative humidity using the rulers, arrow keys or numeric fields. Manual changes save immediately; the top-right circled tick closes the sheet. Use **Edit** on the Indoor card to reopen it.

If a typed reading or plan value is blank or outside its allowed range, the app restores the saved value and explains the range beside the field. Start another edit or adjust its ruler to clear the message. Valid changes continue to save immediately.

## Ventilation plan

Open the ventilation summary below the chart to set minimum indoor temperature, target humidity, room size and window opening. Choose a room preset or enter custom dimensions; custom airflow is also available. These settings save immediately.

## Overview layout

The Settings sheet opened from the Why footer has three groups, starting with **Overview layout**. **Recommendation** is always shown and pinned first, with its chart and ventilation settings. Its lock icon indicates that it cannot be hidden or reordered. Below it, defaults are **Indoor summary** (hidden), **Moisture comparison** (hidden), and **Chart key** (shown). Switch these three boxes on or off; hidden boxes stay in the list. On a touchscreen, hold a dot handle briefly (300ms), then move to reorder. Swiping straight away scrolls the sheet; releasing a hold without moving does nothing. With a mouse or pen, drag normally. Tap or keyboard-activate the handle to open **Move up** and **Move down** text actions.

When the hold is ready, the row highlights. While dragging, the row follows your finger and neighbouring rows move aside to show its destination. The sheet stays still while all reorder rows are visible. If a destination is clipped, pause near the content edge to reveal it. Release to save the move; Escape or an interrupted drag restores its original position. Move menus use the same short settling animation, with reduced-motion settings respected. The menu supports keyboard navigation, disables unavailable moves, and returns focus to the handle after a move or Escape. **Reset** beside the Overview layout heading restores its default order and visibility only. Choices save immediately and survive refreshes.

Older layouts keep the relative order and visibility of the three movable boxes; Recommendation moves to the top and is shown. Credit, Settings and Refresh app are in the Why footer. Use the persistent thermometer button to open Indoor readings, including when both indoor boxes are hidden.

## Indoor entry options

The Indoor summary has icon shortcuts for **camera**, **microphone** when enabled and supported, and **pencil** for manual editing. Camera opens scanning directly and requires review before confirming readings. The **Indoor readings** Settings group contains **Open on launch** (on), **Show camera button** (on), indented **Still photos** (off) and **Auto flash** (on) options, then **Show voice button** (off). Camera and voice visibility applies to both summary shortcuts and sheet entry buttons. Turning **Show camera button** off hides **Still photos** and **Auto flash**, including their descriptions. Their saved values remain independent and are restored when the camera button is enabled.

The camera-help Settings link focuses Auto flash when visible, otherwise Show camera button. Hiding entry buttons does not interrupt an open flow or remove Retake. The top-right circled tick stays visible while the Settings options scroll. It closes Settings; changes already save immediately. If browser storage is blocked, choices remain effective for the current page session.

## Appearance

The last Settings group, **Appearance**, follows **Indoor readings**. Choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. System is the default and follows your device's appearance, including changes while the app is open. Light and Dark keep their chosen appearance independently of the device. The choice saves immediately, survives refresh and applies before the interface appears. **Reset** in Overview layout changes layout only and keeps your appearance choice.

Dark appearance covers both pages, sheets, controls and feedback. Recommendation gradients and the chart's white, amber and coral meanings stay the same. Browser toolbar colours and the installed app's launch screen retain their existing colours; those areas may differ from the chosen appearance.

## Closing sheets and navigating

Sheets can be closed with Escape, a tap outside, or their header controls, which stay visible on phones and desktop. Settings, manual Indoor readings and Ventilation settings use a circled tick; Location and Timer use a circled X. Below 640px, drag down from the handle or header to dismiss. Focus returns to the opener without a highlight unless its keyboard focus indicator was visible when the sheet opened. The Why tab contains **Why this recommendation?**, **Glossary**, **How estimates work** and **Weather data**. **Chart key** remains on Overview. The separate circular thermometer button (**Indoor**) to the right of the tabs opens manual readings from either page without switching pages.

Tap the bottom tabs to switch instantly, or swipe between views; slow swipes must pass halfway and deliberate fast flicks can switch sooner.

Settings, Indoor readings, Ventilation settings, Location and Ventilation timer use the available phone screen height before scrolling. Their title and drag handle remain visible at the top, including during camera capture/review and voice input. All sheet headers sit outside scrolling content, and scrollbars stay in the content area. Settings, manual Indoor readings and Ventilation settings keep their header tick visible; Ventilation timer keeps Start visible. Camera and voice retain their own actions. Location fits its current content instead of reserving empty results space. The Unreleased sheets use tinted glass backgrounds with 4px blur, at 95% white in Light and 55% tint in Dark. The bottom menu remains at 55% in both themes. The glass background is continuous through the header until scrolling content reaches it; the stationary header then covers and blurs that content. Existing fixed actions keep their glass background. There are no fades. The Timer Start action remains pinned at the bottom; scrollbars stay between the pinned areas.

Focused fields stay visible above the keyboard; the header tick/X, Back and confirmation actions retain their usual save or cancellation behaviour.

Across the app, swiping up, down or sideways does not also press a button, toggle a setting or open a link. A touch that stops scrolling does not activate the control underneath. Tap again once scrolling has stopped. Deliberate chart inspection, ruler adjustments, camera cropping and reordering keep their normal gestures; keyboard actions remain available.
